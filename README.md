# MetroForge 3D — City Builder & Macroeconomic Simulation

An interactive 3D city-building and real-world macroeconomic simulation built with **Three.js** and vanilla JavaScript.

## Features
- **3D Procedural World & Day/Night Cycle:** Dynamic lighting, shadows, weather states (Clear, Overcast, Rain), and glowing night windows/streetlights.
- **Living Citizens & Multi-Type Traffic:** Animated pedestrian NPCs with profiles and street-level follow camera, sedans, buses, freight trucks, cargo ships, birds, and Code-3 emergency vehicles.
- **Zoning & Infrastructure:** Asphalt roads & bridges, residential/commercial/industrial zones that upgrade into high-rises, utilities (Wind, Solar, Coal, Water Treatment), civic services (Police, Fire, Hospitals, Universities), and Global Trade Hubs.
- **Macroeconomic & Commodities Engine:** Adjust RCI tax rates, Central Bank interest rates, Municipal Bonds, public service funding, and trade global commodities with live SVG sparkline charts.
- **Save & Export:** LocalStorage auto-save/load plus JSON city export/import.

## Quick Start
1. Open `index.html` directly in any modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) — no build step required.
2. Or serve locally:
   ```bash
   npx serve .
   ```
